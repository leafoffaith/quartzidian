Enzia Investment Thesis: Toy Chest <Working Title/>

India's toy industry is at an inflection point. It is expected to reach USD 4.7 billion by 2033, growing at a CAGR of approximately 10%, outpacing global estimates of approximately 6%. Rising disposable incomes, breakneck urbanisation, widespread digital penetration are fuelling growth in a market that remains highly fragmented yet structurally underpenetrated with untapped potential for growth and immense headroom for scaled, branded players.

Parents are increasingly gravitating towards educational toys to enhance *experimental learning experiences* and outcome *oriented play*, a trend that has been driving the Indian toys market. In the last 7 to 8 years, 150+ organised toy and edutainment companies have emerged across STEM kits, educational games, smart toys, and learning-focused play systems. Beyond consumer toys, early signals are emerging in B2B educational no-code platforms that enable teachers and institutions to design, localize, and distribute play-based learning experiences, representing a potential ‘infrastructure layer’ for the edutainment ecosystem.
