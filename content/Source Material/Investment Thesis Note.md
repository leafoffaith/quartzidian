### Intro 

The global gaming industry is undergoing a paradigm shift with the advent of cloud gaming – a technology that allows high-quality games to be streamed seamlessly on various devices without the need for expensive hardware. This shift is particularly evident in India, a country witnessing rapid digital transformation driven by increasing smartphone penetration and affordable internet access.

Projected revenues in India's cloud gaming market are expected to reach US$164.50 million by 2025, showcasing the immense potential of this sector. With an impressive annual growth rate (CAGR) of 26.82% from 2025 to 2029, the market volume is anticipated to hit US$425.50 million by 2029. Furthermore, the number of users is set to increase to 28.6 million by 2029, underlining the expanding consumer base for cloud gaming in India.

Despite these promising figures, user penetration remains relatively low at 1.8% in 2025, expected to slightly increase to 1.9% by 2029. However, this presents a significant opportunity for growth and expansion. The average revenue per user (ARPU) is projected to be US$6.32, further highlighting the monetization potential of this burgeoning market.

In the global context, the United States leads in terms of revenue generation, with projections reaching US$2,904.00 million in 2025. Yet, the unique dynamics of the Indian market – characterized by its vast population, growing middle class, and digital savviness – make it a compelling landscape for cloud gaming.

As we delve deeper into this investment thesis, we will explore the key drivers of India's cloud gaming market, assess the competitive landscape, and identify strategic opportunities for investment in this exciting and innovative sector.
