In 2025, the Punjab Government launched an ambitious initiative to operationalise 2,000+ *Pind Clinics* to provide "last-mile" primary healthcare which they intend to see through 2026. As the Senior Associate for Data & Monitoring, I led the foundational efforts to solve a massive accessibility gap.

By leveraging geospatial intelligence and cross-functional stakeholder management, I transformed raw electoral and census data into a strategic roadmap, identifying high-need "healthcare deserts" and implementing a rigorous tracking system to ensure quality at scale from day one.
  
**Strategic Interventions**

Preparing to launch over 2,000 clinics wasn’t just a logistics hurdle, it was a coordination challenge across different tiers of the government. Three friction points were identified to successfully navigate complex government coordination:

_1._ _Establishing A Single Source of Truth:_ There was a scarcity of health department data, local administrative maps and census data showing where a clinic should go. 
	_a._ _Solution:_ Acted as the **central product node**. Synthesised three disparate datasets in what proved to be essential in moving from anecdotal decision-making to data-backed strategy relying on free and publicly available datasets for visualisations alleviating the information silo.

_2._ _Removing Operational Bottlenecks:_ Even after identification, resource allocation was often blocked because of a lack of visibility of the “readiness” of a clinic.
	_a._ _Solution:_ Designed & developed a **continuous monitoring loop** by collecting data for 1000+ clinics opening by March with Google Forms and creating a pipeline to internal Dashboards. Thus, revealing readiness and requirements of essential resources to unblock them before the scheduled launch date.
	_b._ _KPI:_ Developed 80 indicators to keep track of readiness and unblock “stalled” clinics 30 days before the scheduled launch

_3._ _Data-driven Prioritisation:_ Refocusing efforts on actual last mile needs instead of solely political visibility.  Several portions of the population had to travel > 10km for basic care.
	_a._ _Solution:_ By overlaying electoral data with distance-to-nearest-clinic metric, I was able to demonstrate why “Site A” would serve disproportionately more people that “Site B”  
  
 **The Impact** 

- Through this three-pronged approach, we were able to reprioritize over 200 clinics in the first weeks of January alone.
- Streamlined the operational blueprint of 1000+ clinics throughout all districts of Punjab for the first phase of the *Pind Clinic* development plan.
- The **80-indicator framework** now serves as the gold standard for tracking clinic readiness and quality assurance across the state.
