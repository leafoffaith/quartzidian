<u>Hacking</u>
	Exploiting vulnerabilities 
	<mark class="hltr-pink">Black-Hat</mark>: Steal money/personal gains
	<mark class="hltr-pink">White-Hat</mark>: Use same techniques to find vulnerabilities with permission
	<mark class="hltr-pink">Grey-Hat</mark>: Don't do it for personal gain but for fun, but it is still illegal

<u>Ethical Hacking v. Penetration Testing</u> 
Ethical hackers have permission of the owner, signs an NDA with the employer/owner of the business 

Penetration Testing is of a more narrow scale, identify what attackers will do if they gain access and try to provide remedy and fixes. Provide patches

<mark class="hltr-cyan">Blue Team</mark>: It staff employed by the organization 
<mark class="hltr-orange">Red Team</mark>: Hackers employed to see how the organization will respond 

<u>Black-Box Testing</u>
	Zero Knowledge hacking to test the system 
	an example of this system is blind testing
	there is also **double blind testing** blue team does not know that red team is attacking
	target testing to test certain vulnerabilities
