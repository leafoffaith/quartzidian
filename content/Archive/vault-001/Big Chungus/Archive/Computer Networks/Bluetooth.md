	Any communication is between the master and a slave

PROS
	Low cost
	Easy to use 
	Pen through walls
	Creates ad-hoc connection without wire
	Used for voice and data transfer
CONS
	It can be hacled and hence, less secure
	It has a slow data transfer rate
	It has small range: 10 meters
