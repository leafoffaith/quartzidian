  
There are two basic types of broadcast networks:  
	• Satellite networks. These have a very long delay  
	(about 270 msec). The protocols used on satellite  
	channels are called Aloha. There are, of course,  
	several variations.  
	• Local area networks (LAN). These have a very short  
	delay. Protocols are called CSMA (Carrier Sense  
	Multiple Access), again with several variations.
