Whenever an electrical current passes through a wire, a magnetic field is created around it. The opposite is also true where when a magnetic field is around a wire, an electrical current is produced in it. 

Any circuit is made of 2 wires, negative and positive voltage. The polarity of electrical current and magnetic field is opposite. Placing the wires close to one another will reduce the noise because the magnetic fields will cancel

If the wires are twisted, will now alt positions along the length of the cable reducing cross talk where the signal on one wire induced onto a nearby wire.  Prevents incoming noise

Magnetic noise will be as close to the signal thus near complete cancellation
