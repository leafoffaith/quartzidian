*P2P*
It could be simplex which would mean just one direction
Full duplex, both direction
Half duplex, both direction but not send and receive at the same time 

It benefits from being simple in design
But it's also a drawback because it can only support 2 nodes at once

*Star*
There is one node that connects to the rest. If one connecting node wants to connect to the other, which can be a hub a switch or a router or whatever, it has to go through the central node. 

What are the advantages of Star topology?
	Failure of connecting nodes doesn't affect everything else but if the central node does go down, everything else is busted

*Tree*
In this case there are multiple nodes that are more important than the rest
There's a hierarchy of nodes and you don't need to be connected to the one at the top, per se

*Mesh*
All nodes are connected to every other node in a **complete mesh** but there might be a **partial mesh** where one node may be connected to multiple nodes but not every node
