**Consistency** 
	Stick with the same colour scheme 
**Animations & Sounds** 
	Don't make it too visually stimulating 
**Clicking & Scrolling** 
	You need crucial information to be super accessible 
**Colours & Contrasts**
	Use complementary colours
	Don't use colours similar to each other lest they look the same - visually 

While analysing HCI systems, don't be subjective and vague
Like, '__ wasn't easy to use' **isn't good** 
but '__ wasn't easy to use because of __' is a better way to put it
