This will be present on the [[Main Navbar]] and on clicking this, it will first check if the user isLoggedin
If not then they will be redirected to the login/sign up page

After that they will be redirected to their individual list where they can add the albums they want to listen to in the future. (Things they plan on listening to)

Here, they can manually add the album name and all
OR they can use a Spotify album link to add it

The `list item` will have a link to the Spotify album (if it exists so check if it exists in Spotify)

After signing in the navbar will now also have a `sign out option in case the user wants to end the session`
