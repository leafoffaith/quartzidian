### A brief inquiry into why I've started blogging

In short: [this](https://www.benkuhn.net/) inspired me to start writing  
  
I’ve often been told that I *should* start writing and I wonder if that’s because I write well or if people would rather have me expend my energy typing out my thoughts instead. Regardless, I am moving to a new city for work and I’ll be alone there. I’m not particularly worried that I’ll feel lonely there. I’ve always liked being at home, close to family and friends but I went abroad for my MSc and it was fun experiencing an entirely different culture. More on that in a future post.  
  
In this case, though, I want to document my life in detail as I traverse uncharted territory, not to combat loneliness but be more aware of what I call ‘home’. Is it a physical space, a set of walls, a state of mind or this keyboard I type on?  
  
It wouldn’t be a brief enquiry if I rambled on right now  :)
