Observations and Proposed changes

A focused usability audit and user behavior analysis reveal several friction points in the current app journey. These barriers—primarily around discoverability, delayed personalization, and shallow engagement tools—risk dampening user conversion and long-term retention. To enhance user experience while driving core business metrics, a series of small-to-medium product enhancements are proposed for implementation over the next sprint cycles.

## **Key Observations**

A critical finding is the lack of immediate personalization during onboarding. While the app has a technically sound sign-up system using phone numbers, it misses an opportunity to gather meaningful user inputs early on—such as skin type, primary concerns, or goals—which would help tailor the app experience from the first session. The flagship personalization experience, the Skin Horoscope, is valuable but buried too deep in the flow. Most users encounter it after several clicks, weakening its impact.

The AR-based skin analysis feature, though a technical differentiator, is under-leveraged at the point of onboarding. Given that users often expect instant utility from beauty tech apps, surfacing this tool earlier could significantly improve both perceived and actual personalization.

Search and product discovery also surfaced as friction-heavy. Users face challenges with typo-sensitive search, limited auto-suggestions, and a lack of contextual filters. Moreover, essential product information such as SKU codes or usage details is either missing or not prominently displayed on product cards and collection pages.

From a behavioral standpoint, engagement tools like reminders, habit tracking, and favorite product saves are either absent or underutilized. These features are essential for improving daily active use and forming user habits. Additionally, current uninstall behavior is not monitored, missing a crucial opportunity to capture user exit intent or offer retention incentives.

## **Proposed Changes**

To tackle these issues systematically, a pipeline of feature changes and UX optimizations can be designed to address three major pillars: engagement, conversion, and retention.

1. **Improved Onboarding with Early Personalization**  
    Introduce a lightweight personalization flow right after sign-up, integrating the AR skin analysis tool and a goal-selection screen. This offers value immediately and helps route users to relevant routines and products.
    
2. **Enhanced Discoverability of Skin Horoscope and AR Tool**  
    Shift the Skin Horoscope prompt higher in the homepage scroll and add a floating button for “Quick Personalize,” improving visibility and access. A carousel featuring popular skin profiles can be added for first-time users.
    
3. **Smarter Search Experience**  
    Implement fuzzy search, auto-correction for common misspellings, and contextual search suggestions based on trending keywords, skin concerns, and previous user behavior.
    
4. **Product Information Enhancements**  
    Add SKU details, badges for "New" or "Best Seller," and a one-tap “Add to Cart” button on product cards to reduce friction in the browsing-to-cart journey.
    
5. **Micro-engagement Features for Habit Formation**  
    Enable daily skincare reminders, in-app tips, and a simple “Skin Journal” to track progress over time. These features not only keep users returning but also reinforce the value of consistent skincare.
    
6. **Retention and Uninstall Reduction Features**  
    Introduce a quick exit survey during uninstalls to understand drop-off points, paired with a loyalty-driven retention offer. Additionally, a points-based reward system can incentivize reviews, referrals, and repeat purchases.
    
7. **UI Improvements for First Fold and Loading**  
    Reduce the splash screen animation delay to under 500ms and embed a GIF-based product banner in the first fold to instantly capture attention. Consider adding a more prominent call to action. 
    

These changes aim to drive meaningful improvements in FoxTale’s conversion funnel, especially in early onboarding and add-to-cart behavior, while also introducing low-friction mechanisms for habit-building and retention.

**Conclusion**

FoxTale’s current app has strong technical and conceptual foundations, including an AR-based skin analysis tool that stands out in the skincare app space. However, with minor UX enhancements and a sharper focus on early-stage personalization and product discovery, the brand can significantly uplift engagement, conversion, and retention. The proposed roadmap offers high-impact changes with manageable development overhead, setting the stage for deeper user trust and long-term brand loyalty.
