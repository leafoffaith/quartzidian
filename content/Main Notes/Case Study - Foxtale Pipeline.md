# FoxTale Skincare App Case Study: Driving Engagement, Conversion & Retention

---

## Client Overview & Context

FoxTale is a direct-to-consumer skincare brand offering highly personalized routines powered by two core innovations:

1. **Skin Horoscope** – a proprietary algorithm that delivers tailored product recommendations based on user skin-profile inputs.
    
2. **AR-Based Skin Analysis** – an interactive feature that uses on-device augmented reality to assess skin texture, hydration, and redness in real time.
    

A recent usability audit and user research uncovered opportunities to make onboarding smoother, surface personalization earlier, and improve search and feature discoverability. As a UX/Product candidate, I proposed an 8-week roadmap of bite-sized, high-impact features to boost three key metrics: **engagement**, **conversion**, and **retention**.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

1. **Onboarding personalization lag**: While signup (phone or email) is seamless, neither the Skin Horoscope nor AR scan prompts appear until after several taps.
    
2. **Hidden personalization flows**: Users must navigate deep into menus to access their AR scan results or horoscope insights.
    
3. **Search limitations**: No typo correction, sparse filters, and missing SKU details hinder product discovery.
    
4. **Lack of engagement boosters**: Essential features—favorites, reminders, progress tracking—are absent or hard to find.
    

### Key Objectives & Success Metrics (KPIs)

- **Engagement**: Lift DAU/WAU/MAU and session duration with interactive elements and nudges.
    
- **Conversion**:
    
    - Increase install-to-login rate by embedding personalization earlier.
        
    - Improve view-to-add-to-cart rate with better discovery tools.
        
- **Retention**:
    
    - Enhance 7-day and 30-day return rates through habit-forming features.
        
    - Reduce uninstall rate via exit insights and targeted offers.
        

---

## 2. Strategic Approach & Prioritization

### Impact vs. Effort Matrix

- **High Impact / Low Effort**: Skin-type filters, fuzzy search, SKU badges.
    
- **Medium Impact / Medium Effort**: Push reminders, one-click buy, loyalty points.
    
- **High Impact / Medium Effort (leveraging existing AR)**: Elevate AR-based analysis by surfacing real-time scan prompts in the onboarding flow and linking insights directly to personalized routines.
    

### Scoring Criteria

- **Complexity**: Low / Medium / High
    
- **Metric Impact**: Low / Medium / High
    
- **Timeline**: Sprints 1–4

---

## 4. Anticipated Impact & Success Metrics

### Conversion

- **+15–20%** uplift in view-to-add-to-cart within the first month.
    

### Engagement

- **+10%** DAU increase by surfacing AR scan and horoscope earlier.
    
- **+20%** longer sessions from interactive banners and scan prompts.
    

### Retention

- **+5%** boost in 7-day retention via push reminders and favorites.
    
- **-8%** reduction in uninstall rate after exit surveys.
    

### Measurement Plan

- A/B test personalization touchpoints (onboarding AR vs. control).
    
- Monitor event funnels in Amplitude for scan prompts, add-to-cart, and repeat sessions.
    
- Weekly dashboards tracking CTR, scan completion, and retention cohorts.
    

---

## 5. Conclusion & Next Steps

This roadmap combines **quick wins** and **strategic enhancements**—notably, maximizing the impact of FoxTale’s existing AR-based skin analysis—to accelerate engagement, conversion, and retention. Moving forward, we’ll iterate on AI-driven routines, deeper AR diagnostics, and third-party wellness integrations.

### Final Reflection

Aligning small, measurable features with clear success metrics and leveraging existing AR capabilities ensures FoxTale can deliver a truly personalized, habit-forming skincare experience that retains users and maximizes lifetime value.
