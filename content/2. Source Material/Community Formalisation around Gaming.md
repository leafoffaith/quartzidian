Community Formalisation around Gaming

Observation: There is a growing trend of formalized gaming communities in urban areas like Delhi. These communities, which started with board games, have expanded to include various clubs focused on Tabletop Role-Playing Games (TTRPGs), Trading Card Games (TCGs), Social Deduction games, and more.
    
Personal Experience: You've personally witnessed the rise of these gaming communities in Delhi. What began as casual board game sessions has evolved into structured clubs catering to different types of games. These clubs attract a regular inflow of participants, fostering a sense of community and shared experiences around gaming.
    
### Supporting Data:
    
While there may not be specific data on the growth of gaming communities in Delhi, it's part of a broader global trend. For instance, Meetup.com lists over 4,000 board game groups worldwide, reflecting the popularity of community-based gaming.
    
The success of Indian board games like Shasn also indicates a growing interest in community gaming experiences. Shasn raised over $350,000 on Kickstarter, showing strong support from the gaming community (source: Kickstarter).
    
According to a report by Mordor Intelligence, the global market for TCGs is expected to grow at a CAGR of 3% from 2021 to 2026, suggesting a sustained interest in community-oriented games.
    
### Significance:
    
The formalization of gaming communities creates opportunities for monetization through event hosting, membership fees, merchandise sales, and partnerships with game publishers.
    
These communities foster social interaction and engagement, enhancing player retention and word-of-mouth promotion.
    
Developers of board games, TTRPGs, and TCGs can leverage these communities to test new games, gather feedback, and build a loyal fanbase.
    
The rise of such communities also contributes to the broader gaming ecosystem, supporting related sectors like esports, live game streaming, and gaming influencers.
