## Mobile Gaming Reigns Supreme in india

Observation: The majority of gamers in India are playing games on mobile devices. This trend is driven by the widespread accessibility and affordability of smartphones, coupled with the increasing availability of high-quality mobile games.
    
Personal Experience: You may have noticed that most people around you prefer to play games on their mobile devices due to the convenience and flexibility it offers. Whether it's on the commute, during breaks, or at home, mobile gaming fits seamlessly into people's daily routines.
    
### Supporting Data:
    
India is the world's second-largest mobile gaming market

About 66% of gamers were from non-metro cities, with 43% of gamers being first-time earners in the 18-30 age group. 148 million users make in-game purchases, with 8 million new paying players added in FY24. In-app purchases, propelled by mid-core games, grew by 41% year-on-year, and continues to be the fastest growing slice out of the $3.8 Bn revenue pie for FY 24. India is the world’s second-largest mobile gaming market, recording 15.2 billion downloads - 3x the combined volume of Brazil and the US,” said the report.

According to a report by KPMG, India had over 365 million mobile gamers in 2020, and this number is expected to reach 510 million by 2022.
    
As per the same report, mobile gaming revenue in India is projected to reach $1.68 billion by 2025, indicating strong monetization potential.
    
The ORF report you provided also emphasizes that mobile gaming dominates the Indian gaming landscape, largely due to the high penetration of affordable smartphones and cheap data plans.
    
### **Significance**:
    
The dominance of mobile gaming opens up massive opportunities for game developers and publishers. They can cater to a vast audience by focusing on mobile-first strategies and creating games optimized for mobile platforms.
    
Given the price sensitivity of the Indian market, free-to-play (F2P) models with in-app purchases (IAPs) are particularly effective for monetizing mobile games.
    
The rise of mobile gaming also fuels demand for localized content and culturally relevant games, as developers aim to capture diverse audiences across different regions of India.
    
Furthermore, the popularity of mobile gaming has led to the growth of related sectors like esports, live game streaming, and gaming influencers, creating an entire ecosystem around mobile gaming.
